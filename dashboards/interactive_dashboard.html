Strategic Insights & Executive Recommendations

1. Restructure Heavy Discounts on Furniture

Tables (3.66% margin) and Bookcases (3.79% margin) are suffering from deep discount bleed. Implement an automated discount ceiling at 15% for Furniture to protect bottom-line profits.

2. Scale Technology Product Bundling

Technology generates 69.1% ($4.97M) of gross revenue with healthy 32%+ margins. Bundle top-selling laptops (MacBook Pro, ThinkPad X1) with high-margin peripherals and office accessories.

3. Optimize Central Region Distribution Costs

While the East ($555k profit) and South ($563k profit) lead in profitability, the Central region lags at $494k. Audit fulfillment logistics and supply chain freight costs in the Midwest.

4. Expand High-Margin Office Supplies

Office supplies (Binders, Storage, Art, Paper) boast industry-leading margins of ~40%. Leverage targeted corporate subscriptions to turn these into recurring, predictable margin boosters.
